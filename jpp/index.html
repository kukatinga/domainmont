How to use these predictions

Nobody predicts all 17. The money is in the bonus tiers — 12 to 16 correct on the Mega, 10 to 12 on the Midweek — so the slip is built to maximise those, not a perfect card.

Banker

The games with the strongest probability. Anchor your slip on these and don't double-chance them away.

Upset watch

Volatile games where the crowd tends to get it wrong. Consider the single pick here, or cover it with a double chance.

Building a multi-line (perm) slip

Add a double chance to a toss-up game and your lines double: 1 DC = 2 lines, 2 DCs = 4, 3 DCs = 8. Use the lines & cost calculator to see the price before you commit, and keep DCs for the upset-watch games rather than the bankers.

Where the fixtures come from

Fixtures are pulled from SportPesa's live feed (17 for the Mega, 13 for the Midweek), so the list updates itself when the jackpot changes. If a match is postponed, SportPesa's own rules decide how it is treated — check there before the games kick off.

Which jackpot is this page about?

SportPesa's Mega Jackpot and Midweek Jackpot only. Betika, Mozzart and other jackpots have different fixtures and aren't covered here.

Mega Jackpot — frequently asked

How many games are in the SportPesa Mega Jackpot?

Seventeen. You predict 1, X, or 2 for each.

Do I need all 17 Mega Jackpot games right to win?

No. Bonuses start from 12 correct. All 17 wins the full jackpot.

When does the Mega Jackpot close?

Betting closes before the first jackpot match kicks off. The countdown at the top of the page shows the time left for this week's slip.

When are the Mega predictions published?

Picks are published each Tuesday before the weekend fixtures, and rechecked closer to kickoff.

What does 1, X and 2 mean?

1 is a home win, X is a draw, 2 is an away win. A double chance covers two of the three outcomes in a single game.

How do I get all 17 Mega picks?

The first picks on this page are free. For the full slip, pay KES 99 by M-Pesa using the prompt on this page and the picks arrive by SMS.

Midweek Jackpot — frequently asked

How many games are in the SportPesa Midweek Jackpot?

Thirteen. You predict 1, X, or 2 for each.

Do I need all 13 Midweek games right to win?

No. Bonuses start from 10 correct. All 13 wins the full jackpot.

When is the Midweek Jackpot drawn?

On Wednesday. Picks are published each Sunday before the midweek fixtures.

How do I get all 13 Midweek picks?

Use the M-Pesa prompt on this page — KES 99. Picks arrive by SMS before kickoff.
