How It Works

Three layers before every Mega and Midweek slip is published.

1. Live fixture feed

Every fixture is pulled directly from the SportPesa feed — 17 for the Mega, 13 for the Midweek. No manual entry, no stale lists, no guessing.

2. Bankers + Upsets

Three banker picks on the strongest-probability games to anchor the slip. Then we hunt for upsets — the volatile, high-value games where the crowd is likely to get it wrong. The goal is to maximise your hits on the bonus tiers — 12/17 to 16/17 for the Mega, 10/13 to 12/13 for the Midweek — not to chase the impossible full house.

3. SMS in about 5 seconds

Pay via M-Pesa. The full slip lands by SMS. No app, no login, no subscription.

Why This Approach

The Mega needs 17 correct calls. Random chance puts that at roughly 1 in 129 million. The Midweek needs 13, which is easier but still a lottery ticket if you pick blind.

The games are not equally hard — some are heavily skewed, others are near coin flips. The edge is knowing which games to be conservative on (the bankers), and where to go against the crowd (the upsets).

Nobody "predicts" a perfect slip. The real money is in the bonus tiers. A 16/17 or a 12/13 pays millions and is far more achievable than the full house. That is what both slips are built for.
