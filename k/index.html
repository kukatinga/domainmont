How It Works

From API key to live trading in minutes

No infrastructure to manage. No PhD required. Connect, configure, and run.

01

Connect Your Kalshi Account

Generate a read/write API key in your Kalshi dashboard and paste it into KalshiBot. Your funds never leave your account.

02

Pick or Build a Strategy

Choose from our library of pre-built strategies — momentum, mean reversion, news-driven — or write your own using our Python SDK.

03

Set Risk Parameters & Go Live

Define your max position size, drawdown limit, and daily loss cap. Then hit start. KalshiBot runs 24/7 from our cloud servers.
