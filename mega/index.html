What is the SportPesa Mega Jackpot?

The SportPesa Mega Jackpot is a weekly pool of 17 pre-selected football matches. You predict the result of each game: 1 for a home win, X for a draw, 2 for an away win. A single bet costs KES 99. You play through the SportPesa app or website. The jackpot closes before the first kickoff of the round, usually on a weekend afternoon. If nobody predicts all 17 correctly, the top prize rolls over and grows the following week.

How the Bonus Prizes Work

You do not need all 17 correct to win. SportPesa pays bonuses from 12 correct predictions upward. The tiers run through 12, 13, 14, 15, and 16 correct, with the full jackpot for 17. Bonus amounts vary by week depending on how many players hit each tier and the size of the pool. A "rolled over" jackpot means the top prize was not won in the previous round, so it carries forward and increases.

How to Play, Step by Step

  1. Log into your SportPesa account on the app or website.
  2. Open the Mega Jackpot section.
  3. For each of the 17 games, tap 1, X, or 2.
  4. Check your bet slip. A single entry shows one combination.
  5. Confirm the bet. The stake is KES 99.
  6. Keep your bet ID. Results are published after the final match.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many games are in the Mega Jackpot?

Seventeen. You predict 1, X, or 2 for each of the 17 pre-selected fixtures.

How much does a bet cost?

KES 99 per bet. Multiple entries cost KES 99 each.

Do I need all 17 right to win?

No. Bonuses start from 12 correct predictions. The top jackpot requires 17, but 12 through 16 all pay.

When does it close?

Before the first kickoff of the round, usually on a weekend. Check the countdown on the live block above.

When are results out?

After the final match of the 17. SportPesa publishes results and bonus tiers on its jackpot page.

Are your predictions guaranteed?

No. No prediction is guaranteed. We publish our record honestly and you should treat every bet as entertainment, not income.
